# Anti AIGC Rewriter

## When To Use

Use after the structure is mostly stable and the user wants academic prose to sound more authored, specific, and logically grounded.

## Workflow

1. Diagnose formulaic openings, repeated sentence rhythm, vague transitions, and generic claims.
2. Preserve facts, evidence, terminology, figure references, and citation intent.
3. Rebuild paragraph logic before changing words.
4. Vary sentence roles: claim, reason, evidence, limitation, transition, and summary.
5. Keep the author's design process and project constraints visible.

## Output

- rewritten passage
- brief change notes
- remaining factual questions if evidence is missing

## Guardrails

Do not make synonym-only edits. Do not invent evidence, project details, survey data, citations, or implementation results.
