# Thesis Structure Audit

## When To Use

Use this before sentence polishing when the thesis feels bloated, repetitive, off-topic, or weakly organized.

## Workflow

1. Read the table of contents, chapter goals, abstract, and assignment requirements.
2. Identify the thesis spine: research object, method, work output, evaluation, and conclusion.
3. Mark each section as keep, merge, move, shrink, rewrite, or delete.
4. Separate structural problems from prose problems.
5. Preserve the CG/art/design main line when cutting unrelated digital-tourism or generic background content.

## Output

- revised outline proposal
- section deletion and merge list
- missing argument links
- conflict or redundancy list
- next-pass handoff notes

## Guardrails

Do not rewrite paragraphs unless asked. Do not invent experiments, sources, project results, or school requirements.
