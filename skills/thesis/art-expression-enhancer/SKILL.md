# Art Expression Enhancer

## When To Use

Use when the thesis reads like a technical manual but should also explain artistic design, theme, audience experience, and visual communication.

## Workflow

1. Identify the project's theme, emotional direction, and intended audience experience.
2. Connect technical choices to visual expression.
3. Strengthen environmental storytelling, shot language, composition, atmosphere, materials, color, rhythm, and interaction.
4. Add design rationale without inflating claims.
5. Keep the work grounded in actual project assets and process.

## Output

- enhanced design-analysis paragraphs
- art-expression angle list
- technical-to-artistic mapping
- missing visual evidence notes

## Guardrails

Do not turn the thesis into vague art praise. Do not invent design intentions, cultural claims, or production details not supported by the project.
